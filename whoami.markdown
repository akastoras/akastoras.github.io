Currently, I work as a Junior Research Engineer at the [Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC-CNS)](https://www.bsc.es/), in the Compilers and Toolchains for HPC team, managed by [Roger Ferrer Ibáñez](https://rofi.roger-ferrer.org/). There I try to improve the support of [LLVM](https://llvm.org/) for the [RISC-V](https://riscv.org/) Vector Extension. Currently I am focusing on the exploitation of the segment instructions.

<!-- #### Performance and Modeling @ Arm France -->
Prior to joining BSC, between Oct 2023 and March 2024, I did my 6-month internship at [Arm France](https://www.arm.com/), in the CPU Performance and Modeling team. There I extended a model of the CPU with a framework for evaluating dead block predictors as the cache replacement policy. My manager was [Jérémy Sauget](https://www.linkedin.com/in/j%C3%A9r%C3%A9my-sauget-55215452/?originalSubdomain=fr).

During my studies I have been working on projects with [Prof. Nikos Bellas](https://csl.e-ce.uth.gr/people/nikolaos-bellas), at the [Computer Systems Lab](https://csl.e-ce.uth.gr/). I have mostly worked with FPGAs and High-Level Synthesis using the Xilinx Vitis HLS platform. I have also worked on the design of [a simple RISC-V processor](https://github.com/akastoras/riscv) in Verilog which was the basis for many student projects at the Parallel Computer Architectures class.
